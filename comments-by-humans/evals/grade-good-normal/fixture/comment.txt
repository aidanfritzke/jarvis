Calls fn until it succeeds and returns its result, sleeping 1s, 2s, 4s between failures so a flaky service gets time to recover instead of being hammered. Takes a zero-argument callable and an attempt count and returns whatever fn returns; nothing in this repo calls it yet, it is meant to wrap calls to flaky services. Catch: it catches every Exception, bugs included, and still sleeps after the final failed attempt before raising RuntimeError.
